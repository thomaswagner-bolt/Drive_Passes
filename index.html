FAQs

How does pass credit work?

Your pass includes a certain amount of credit each month to use on Bolt Drive rentals. Pass credit only applies to vehicles operated by Bolt Drive.

Pass credit expires at the end of each month. Credit doesn't roll over, doesn't apply to Bolt Balance, and can't be transferred or cashed out.

If you run out of pass credit, your drives charge to your chosen payment method. Your other pass benefits remain active.

If you have a work profile, any pass credit you have will also apply to work drives.

How do pass discounts work?

All passes give you discounts on any pay-as-you-go, hourly, or daily rate. Discounts apply to every Bolt Drive vehicle in Germany. Discounts are applied at the end of your drive.

Pass discounts are valid for the whole month, no matter if you've used all your credit.

Which vehicles qualify for pass discounts?

Pass discounts apply to all Bolt Drive cars and vans in Germany. They cover any pay-as-you-go, hourly, or daily rate.

Pass discounts don't apply to vehicles from partner providers.

How does pass payment work?

Your chosen payment method is charged on the same day every month. You can change how you pay for your pass, or cancel your pass, in the Bolt app. You'll get a monthly invoice via email.

Will my pass renew automatically?

Yes. Your pass automatically renews on the same day every month. You can cancel it at any time in the Bolt app.

Can I pause my pass?

No. Once you buy a pass, you can't pause it. You can always cancel it and renew it again later.

Can I change my pass?

Yes. You can change your pass at any time in the Bolt app. Whether you change to a lower- or higher-priced pass, you'll retain your active pass benefits until the end of the current month. When the next month starts, your pass will update and your benefits will change.

You can only have one active pass at a time.

Can I cancel my pass?

Yes. You can cancel your pass at any time. Once you cancel it, it and its benefits stay active until the end of the month.

You have the option to withdraw an unused pass within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.

How do refunds work?

If you buy a pass and don't use any of its credit, you can cancel it within 14 days for a refund. If you cancel after 14 days, or if you used any of its credit, you won't be refunded. Your pass benefits will be active until the end of the billing period (the month in which you cancelled).

Where is my pass valid?

Your pass is valid anywhere Bolt Drive vehicles are available in Germany. Pass benefits don't apply to vehicles from partner providers.

I have a work profile. Does my pass apply?

Yes. If you have personal and work profiles set up for Bolt Drive, your pass will apply to both of them.

Can I combine a pass and my Bolt Plus subscription?

Yes. Bolt Plus and Drive passes are separate. Both can be active at once.
